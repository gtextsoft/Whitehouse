Buyback option clause

If a client no longer wishes to own the property, the supplied terms allow the client to seek a buyer or request that the company repurchase it.

  1. Buyback price: If the company agrees to repurchase, the price is reduced by 35% of the initial purchase sum agreed when the property was bought.
  2. Payment terms: Payment is made in staggered installments commencing 120 days after signing the Deed of Assignment. Amounts and timing are to be agreed separately.
  3. Company discretion: The company reserves discretion over whether to proceed with a buyback and may discontinue it based on market, financial or other considerations.

Resale and release clauses

  1. Sell option: Previous buyers may seek a buyer and obtain a release form to proceed with resale, including where the full land or house payment is incomplete.
  2. Full payment: A purchaser who has paid in full may resell to a third party after notifying the company and supplying incoming purchaser details. After resale formalities and applicable fees, the company will recognize the new purchaser and issue documentation in their name.
  3. Payment split (optional): If payment is partial, an incoming purchaser may assume the transaction and settle outstanding sums. The company must be notified in advance and given the new purchaser's details for review, approval and record updates. Once formalities, balance and applicable fees are settled, the company processes the transfer and documents.

When agreed payments and terms are fulfilled, GTEXT LAND will transfer ownership rights from the initial purchaser to the new buyer. Both parties are to cooperate with the transfer. The supplied form says this is a general template and that resale agreements may require additional terms; it recommends consulting a legal professional.

Default and revocation

The company reserves the right to revoke a subscription after three months of payment-plan default, with the property deemed forfeited. The client also agrees that plots may be revoked for default on installment payments or other stated fees, with costs incurred from revocation borne by the defaulting party.

Reallocation

The company reserves the right to reallocate a client to another estate if an issue affects subscribed plots, including plot availability or zoning. If the client does not meet the agreed payment schedule before plots in the estate are sold, the company may reallocate to an estate with available plots, subject to its terms and pricing.

Ratification of title

Each client is responsible for ratification and procurement of their individual Governor's Consent under applicable laws and regulations, including associated costs and requirements. The supplied form says the company is not liable for related delays, costs or obligations.
